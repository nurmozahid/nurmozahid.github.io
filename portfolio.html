Nur Mozahid

I specialise in applied econometrics, statistical analysis, economic modelling, and machine learning, transforming complex data into meaningful insights for researchers, businesses, and policymakers.

I am a PhD candidate in the Agricultural Economics and Rural Policy Group at Wageningen University & Research, the Netherlands, and have academic teaching and research experience at Sylhet Agricultural University, Bangladesh.

My research focuses on the economic and environmental sustainability of agri-food systems. I combine production economics, applied econometrics, and machine learning to investigate differences in economic and environmental performance.

My wider expertise includes quantitative research methods, ecosystem valuation, agricultural policy, statistical modelling, and reproducible data analysis using R and Stata.
