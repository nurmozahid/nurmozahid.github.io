Write your biography here. I am a PhD candidate in the Agricultural Economics and Rural Policy Group at Wageningen University & Research, the Netherlands.

My research examines how agricultural production can improve economic performance while reducing its environmental impact. My doctoral research focuses on productivity, economic and environmental efficiency, and pollution abatement costs in Irish dairy farming.

## Research interests

- Agricultural productivity and farm efficiency
- Environmental economics and agricultural policy
- Dairy and livestock economics
- Pollution abatement costs and nutrient management
- Applied econometrics and machine learning

## Teaching

My teaching interests include econometrics, statistics, research methods, and applied data analysis using R and Stata.
