Agricultural & Environmental Economics

Nur Mozahid

I study how agriculture can improve economic performance while reducing its environmental impact.

I am a PhD candidate in the Agricultural Economics and Rural Policy Group at Wageningen University & Research, the Netherlands, and an Assistant Professor at Sylhet Agricultural University, Bangladesh.

My research combines production economics, applied econometrics, and machine learning. I focus on farm productivity, environmental efficiency, and pollution abatement costs, particularly in dairy farming.

Research

My research examines farm performance and the economic implications of environmental improvement.

Farm productivity and efficiency

Understanding differences in agricultural performance and identifying opportunities for economic and environmental improvement.

Environmental economics and policy

Examining nutrient surpluses, emissions, and differences in the costs farms face when reducing pollution.

Applied econometrics and machine learning

Using farm-level data and quantitative methods to understand heterogeneity and inform agricultural decisions.

Teaching

My teaching interests include econometrics, statistics, research methods, and applied data analysis using R and Stata.

I aim to make quantitative methods accessible through practical examples and applications to research questions.

  • Applied econometrics
  • Statistics and quantitative research methods
  • Data analysis using R and Stata
  • Machine learning for applied research
