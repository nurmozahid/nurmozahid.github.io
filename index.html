Agricultural & Environmental Economics

Nur Mozahid

My research focuses on the economic and environmental sustainability of agri-food systems. I use data to identify opportunities to improve productivity, strengthen economic performance, and reduce environmental impacts, generating actionable insights for policymakers and practitioners.

I am a PhD candidate in the Agricultural Economics and Rural Policy Group at Wageningen University & Research, the Netherlands, and former Lecturer at Sylhet Agricultural University, Bangladesh.

I combine production economics, applied econometrics, and machine learning to investigate differences in economic and environmental sustainability/performance. My wider research interests include ecosystem valuation and agricultural policy.

Research

My research examines farm performance and the economic implications of environmental improvement.

Farm productivity and efficiency

Understanding differences in agricultural performance and identifying opportunities for economic and environmental improvement.

Environmental economics and policy

Examining nutrient surpluses, emissions, and differences in the costs farms face when reducing pollution.

Applied econometrics and machine learning

Using farm-level data and quantitative methods to understand heterogeneity and inform agricultural decisions.
